You need the **Consumer** or **Both** persona, and at least one
[enabled consumer repository](/zekt-docs/consumer/enable-repository/).

## Find a service

Go to **Directory → Browse Directory**. Search by service, publisher, tag or event, and narrow the
results with the tag filters. Each card shows the service name, publisher, description, tags and a
[trust badge](/zekt-docs/provider/publish-to-directory/#trust-badges).

You'll see public services, plus Organization-level services from GitHub organizations where your
account has active repositories. If a provider has given you an unlisted service's ID, you can
request access to it too.

## Request access

<Steps>

1. On the service's card, choose **Request access**. You can also start from **Consumer → Requests**.
2. In **Request Access**, choose the **Target Repository**: the consumer repository that should
   receive the events. For a [consumer service](/zekt-docs/consumer/consumer-services/), choose the
   **Source Repository** (one of your provider repositories) and the **Source Workflow** that
   fires instead.
3. Optionally add a **Message** to the provider, and filters: success only, failure only, or
   **Source Branches**. You can only choose branches that the service declares.
4. Choose **Send Request**. The card shows *Request waiting for the provider's approval.*, and
   the request appears under **Consumer → Requests**.

</Steps>

If you have no enabled repository yet, the dialog says *Enable a repository in Zekt Repos tab first.*

### Why a request can be refused

- The service doesn't exist or is disabled ("not found").
- The service is Organization-level, and your account isn't in its audience.
- The service requires [Shield](/zekt-docs/add-ons/shield/), and you haven't uploaded a Shield
  public key.
- Your Shepherd's [Doctrine](/zekt-docs/add-ons/shield/#doctrine-shepherd-accounts) doesn't allow that provider.
- You already have a pending or approved request for the same repository and service.

## After you request

You get an in-app message (under **Profile → Messages**) when the provider decides:

- *Access request approved by @…*, or *…approved automatically by @…*. Events now flow to your
  repository. [Write the receiving workflow](/zekt-docs/consumer/receive-events/) if you haven't.
- *Access request denied by @…*, with the provider's reason. The card shows *The provider denied
  your request.* and offers **Request again**.

You can't withdraw a pending request. You can delete denied or revoked ones.

## Leave a subscription

Go to **Consumer → Subscriptions**, choose **Remove**, then **Confirm Removal**. The subscription
ends at once and the provider is notified. Events that were queued but not yet sent aren't sent.
Events GitHub already accepted still run.

To subscribe again later, request access again.

<Aside type="note">
	Zekt doesn't send email for any of this. Notifications are in-app only and kept for 30 days.
</Aside>
