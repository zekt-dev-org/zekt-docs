## Overview: Provider → Services

**Provider → Services** is a read-only overview of your services. Switch between **Provider
Services** and **Consumer Services** (the latter needs the Both persona). **Add New** opens the
service editor.

| Provider Services column | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| Repository | With a *branch: &lt;name&gt;* badge if the service is limited to a branch. |
| Workflow | A *Self* badge when [self-trigger](/zekt-docs/provider/workflows-and-services/#self-trigger) is on. |
| Description, Tags | Up to 3 tags, then *+N*. |
| Visibility | Public, Organization or Unlisted. |
| Subscribers | How many. |

For consumer services, the columns are Repository, Service Name (with a *disabled* badge), Description,
Tags, Visibility and Direction.

## Make changes: Service Descriptions → Manage Existing

Changes happen in **Provider → Service Descriptions → Manage Existing**. Its columns are Repository,
Workflow, Description, Owner Alias, Tags, Event Type, Visibility, Listed and Actions.

- **Change visibility:** click the Visibility badge. In **Edit Visibility**, choose Public or
  Organization, check the audience preview, and choose **Save Changes**. Every access request still
  needs your approval.
- **List or unlist:** use the **Listed** toggle. Re-listing lists the service at Organization, never
  straight to Public. You can't unlist here while the service has approved consumers.
- **Edit:** the pencil opens the service editor.
- **Delete:** the bin. It's blocked while there are active subscribers (*Active Consumers Detected…
  You must revoke their access before you can delete this service.*). Otherwise you confirm, and the
  service is removed from the Directory, its schemas and avatar are removed, and its pending requests
  are declined.

Provider services have no enable/disable control.

### Consumer services

Under **Service Descriptions → Consumer Services**, each consumer service has **Edit**, **Remove
custom avatar**, **Disable** / **Enable** and **Delete permanently**.

- **Disable** asks for confirmation. A disabled service leaves the Directory and the Registry, refuses
  new requests, can't be used in orchestration, and drops events subscribers fire into it.
- **Delete permanently** is refused while subscribers remain, paused ones included: *Cannot delete:
  N active subscriber(s) remain. Revoke subscriptions first.*

## Subscribers: Provider → Subscribers

**Provider → Subscribers** lists who subscribes to your services, with a summary such as *N active
subscriptions from M consumers* (plus *(K inactive)* for paused ones).

- There's one card per consumer, grouped by your repository, workflow and service, with a status and
  how many of their repositories receive it.
- Click a row to see the consumer repository, the approval date and the status (Active or Paused).
- Ended subscriptions aren't listed. Self-trigger subscriptions are listed in their own section.

<Aside type="note">
	To revoke a subscriber, use **Provider → Access → Revoke Access**. The Subscribers tab has no
	revoke action. See [Handle access requests](/zekt-docs/provider/access-requests/#revoke-access).
</Aside>
