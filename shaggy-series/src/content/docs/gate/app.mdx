The Zekt Gate App, at www.zekt-gate.dev/app, is where you manage Zekt Gate once
[setup](/zekt-docs/gate/overview/#set-it-up) is done.

## Open the App

<Steps>

1. On www.zekt-gate.dev, sign in with Zekt from the header (**Sign in with**, with the Zekt logo).
2. Open the account menu and choose **Zekt Gate App**.

</Steps>

**Zekt Gate App** appears in the menu once the account has at least one connected directory. Until
then, the App tells you your enrollment is still in progress.

**Your enrollments** lists each directory with its tenant ID and its status.

### Directory statuses

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Not started**, **Enrolling** | Enrollment is in progress. |
| **Awaiting administrator** | A directory has been named, and is waiting on its administrator to [approve](/zekt-docs/gate/consent/). |
| **Finishing at Zekt** | The directory proved itself, but Zekt hasn't finished connecting it. |
| **Connected** | Tokens from this directory are accepted. |
| **Disconnected** | Tokens are refused because you [stopped trusting](/zekt-docs/gate/security/#stop-trusting-a-directory) the directory. It doesn't mean the directory was disconnected at Zekt. |
| **Suspended** | Tokens are refused because the Zekt account isn't in good standing. The fix is at Zekt, not in Zekt Gate. |
| **Permission withdrawn** | Tokens are refused because Zekt Gate has recorded that the consent was withdrawn in your directory. |
| **Unknown** | A state the App doesn't recognize. It's treated as stopped. |

## A directory's page

Without signing in to the directory, you see its status, whether its tokens are accepted, the GitHub
organization it's connected to, and **Options**.

### Sign in to the directory

The configuration and call history are behind a sign-in to the directory itself.

<Steps>

1. Under **Prove you still administer this directory**, select **Sign in to this directory at
   Microsoft**.
2. A box then shows the directory, when you proved it, until when, and your **Zekt Gate role**:
   **Administrator** or **None**.

</Steps>

- You don't need a Zekt Gate role to sign in.
- The directory sign-in lasts 15 minutes. After that, sign in again.
- Without the **Zekt Gate Administrator** role you can see the directory's configuration and remove
  applications, but not add one or resume trust. Once someone
  [assigns you the role](/zekt-docs/gate/applications/#choose-who-manages-zekt-gate), select
  **Check again**.

### Who called

Calls to Zekt Gate, with **Date & time**, **App**, **Application (client) ID** and **Status**. Calls
are grouped per minute and kept for 90 days.

### Workflows

One row per service under the directory's GitHub organization. Each row shows **Ready** or
**Not ready**, and **Switched on at Zekt** or **Switched off at Zekt**.

- **Ready** means Zekt reports that the workflow can be started from outside GitHub, every input is
  declared with a type and a description, and the service has a published schema. Zekt Gate can
  then build a form for it: **Preview its form** shows it.
- **Not ready** shows a sentence saying why, and how to fix it. See
  [workflows that aren't ready](/zekt-docs/gate/troubleshooting/#workflows-that-arent-ready).
- **Check again** reads that one service from Zekt again. Use it after you've fixed something.

**Ready** doesn't mean the workflow can be started today. Starting a workflow through Zekt Gate isn't
available yet, and would also need the service to be **Switched on at Zekt**.

### Applications

The applications allowed to call Zekt Gate. The list belongs to your Zekt account and covers every
directory the account has connected. It can hold up to 50 applications.

Each row shows the application's name, its **Application (client) ID**, when it was **Registered**,
and **Seen calling** or **Not yet seen calling**, with **Refresh** and **Remove** buttons.
**Recent changes** lists additions and removals.

To add an application, you need the Zekt Gate Administrator role and the
[**Owner** role](/zekt-docs/operate/members/#roles) on the Zekt account:

<Steps>

1. Under **Add an application**, enter the **Application (client) ID**.
2. Enter **A name you will recognise**, 1 to 64 characters.
3. Select **Add application**.

</Steps>

To remove one, select **Remove**, then **Remove it**. The application can no longer call Zekt Gate
from any directory on the account. It takes effect on the next request. It needs the Owner role on
the Zekt account, but no Zekt Gate role.
If it's the last application on the list, no application can call Zekt Gate afterwards.

### Access

Shows what the directory and each application may reach. It's for display only: it shows limits but
doesn't apply them, and changing access isn't available yet.

### Options

- **Discontinue Zekt Gate** ends Zekt Gate for the directory.
- **Emergency: stop trusting now**, under **For a breach**, stops trusting the directory at once.

Both are explained in [Security](/zekt-docs/gate/security/).
