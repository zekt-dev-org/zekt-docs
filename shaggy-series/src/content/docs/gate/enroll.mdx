Enrolling binds one Entra directory to one GitHub organization on your Zekt account. It's the first
of [three setup steps](/zekt-docs/gate/overview/#set-it-up).

**Who does it:** someone with the [**Owner** role](/zekt-docs/operate/members/#roles) on the Zekt
account who is also an admin of the GitHub organization. One person must hold both.

## Before you start

Get your directory's **tenant ID** from your Entra administrator. In the Microsoft Entra admin
center, it's under **Overview → Tenant ID**. A domain name isn't the same thing.

## Enroll

<Steps>

1. On www.zekt-gate.dev, select **Start enrollment**.
2. You continue on www.zekt.dev. Sign in, choose the Zekt account and the GitHub organization, and
   accept the terms.
3. Zekt Gate gives you a message to forward to your Entra administrator. It contains the approval
   link for [step 2](/zekt-docs/gate/consent/).

</Steps>

Nothing changes in your Entra directory during this step.
