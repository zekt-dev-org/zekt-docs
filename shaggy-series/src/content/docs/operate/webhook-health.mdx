---
title: Webhook health
description: Check and rotate the webhooks Zekt manages on your provider repositories.
---

When you enable a provider repository, Zekt adds a webhook to it so it can see workflow runs. Zekt
creates, secures and removes these webhooks for you. You never handle the webhook secret.

Go to **Health → Webhook Health**. It's available with the Provider or Both persona, and lists the
webhooks on your active provider repositories.

## Ping a webhook

Choose **Ping Webhook**. GitHub sends a test event, and the result is shown. Use it after changes on
GitHub, or when events stop arriving.

## Rotate the secret

Choose **Rotate Secret**. Zekt creates a new webhook secret and updates GitHub.

- Zekt also rotates every secret automatically every **30 days**.
- The tab shows the last rotation, how many rotations there have been, and when the next one is due.
- During a rotation the old and new secrets briefly overlap.

Rotations, including automatic ones and failures, are recorded in the
[audit log](/zekt-docs/operate/audit-log/).
