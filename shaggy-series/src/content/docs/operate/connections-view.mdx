title: Connections and blast radius
description: See who is connected to whom, how healthy each connection is, and what a change would affect.
---

The **Directory** section of the console has three views of your connections.

## My Connections

**Directory → My Connections** lists your connections in two groups, **As Provider** and
**As Consumer**, with a search box. Each has a health dot:

| Dot | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 🟡 Yellow | No events in the last 7 days. This is checked first, so an idle connection is yellow whatever its success rate. |
| 🔴 Red | Success rate below 90%. |
| 🟡 Yellow | Success rate below 98%. |
| 🟢 Green | Otherwise. |

**Details** shows: Success Rate, Total Events, Failed Events, Last Activity, Established and
Workflow Path.

## Brokering

**Directory → Brokering** is an interactive graph of your providers, services and consumers. Switch
between the provider, logical and consumer views, and filter by repository.

## Blast Radius

**Directory → Blast Radius** shows a provider or consumer topology, and lets you run **what-if**
simulations before you change anything. Simulations are read-only and change nothing.

| On a | You can simulate |
| --- | --- |
| Repository | Disable, Delete, Require Shield |
| Workflow | Blacklist, Remove Whitelist |
| Service | Delete, Require Shield |
| Consumer | Revoke Access |

## Chainlink topology

With the Both persona, **Chainlink → Topology** shows event chains through your
[Chainlink](/zekt-docs/add-ons/chainlink/) repositories.
