title: Messages and notifications
description: Where Zekt's in-app notices appear, and how to read and acknowledge them.
---

Zekt notifies you in the console only: it sends no email for these notices.

## What you're notified about

- Notices from Zekt.
- Access requests received, approved or denied; subscriptions ended or unsubscribed.
- A connected account deleted.
- A schema version that doesn't compile.
- The GitHub App uninstalled, suspended, unsuspended or removed from repositories.
- Billing notices, including the trial ending soon.
- Welcome, and a recommendation to rotate your Shield key.

## Profile → Messages

Open it from **Profile → Messages**, or the avatar menu → **Notifications & Messages**.

- The header shows **Total** and **Unread** counts.
- **Search messages…** searches subject, body and sender.
- Filter by **Type** (All, Info, Warning, Critical) and **Status** (All, Unread, Acknowledged).
- Messages are newest first. Click one to expand it. An orange dot marks unread messages, and
  **✓ Read** marks read ones.
- Use **Mark as Read** to clear one.

Notifications stop being highlighted after 30 days, but stay in your message list.

## Notification bubble and avatar dot

- A **bubble** at the bottom right shows how many unread messages you have, and opens them one by one.
  Choose **Acknowledge**, or **Remind Me Later (4 hours)**. Critical messages don't offer *Remind Me
  Later*.
- A **dot** on your avatar, and on the *Notifications & Messages* menu item, shows there's something
  unread. It's checked when the console opens and about once a minute.
