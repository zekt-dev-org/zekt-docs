Both live in a provider's **trust modal**. Open it by clicking the provider's
[trust badge](/zekt-docs/provider/publish-to-directory/#trust-badges): in the Directory, or under
Requests, Subscriptions or Health.

## Reviews

A review is about a **provider account**, not a single service. Write one in the trust modal's
**Testimonials** tab with **Write a Review**:

- an overall rating of 1–5 stars (required);
- **Reliability**, **Communication**, **Documentation** and **Value for Money**, 1–5 each. These
  start at 3, so set them deliberately;
- optional text, up to 2,000 characters.

**Who can review:** only an account that holds, or once held, an approved subscription to that
provider. You can't review yourself.

- You have one review per provider. Submitting again replaces it, and **Withdraw my review** deletes
  it.
- Reviews aren't moderated, and appear immediately.
- Signed-in Zekt users see them in that provider's trust modal. The reviewer's name isn't shown, and
  only reviews with text are listed.
- Reviews don't appear on Directory cards or the public Registry, and don't change the trust badge.

**✓ Verified** on a review means the reviewer has an **active** subscription to the provider,
approved by the provider by hand or by a rule for that specific user (not by the *All Consumers*
rule). It's checked each time the review is shown. It's not the same as the provider's
*GitHub Verified* trust badge.

## Abuse reports

Use the trust modal's **Report Abuse** tab to report a provider. Any signed-in account can report
another account, but not its own.

| Field | Details |
| --- | --- |
| **Type** | Impersonation, Trademark, Spam, Fraud, Inappropriate Content or Other. |
| **Description** | Required, up to 5,000 characters. |
| **Evidence URL** | Optional, `http` or `https`. |
| **Contact email** | How to reach you. |

- You get a **Report ID**. The Zekt team reviews reports.
- No review time is promised, there's no status page, and you aren't notified of the outcome.
- Reports are never shown publicly.
- Limits: 5 reports per minute, and one open report per provider (*You already have an open
  report…*).

<Aside type="note">
	When an account is deleted, the reviews and abuse reports it was involved in are kept as described
	in [Delete your account](/zekt-docs/operate/account/#what-happens-after-deletion).
</Aside>
