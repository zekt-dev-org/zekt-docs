There are two ways for several people to work with Zekt under one bill:

- **Members** share **one account**. Everyone works on the same repositories and services.
- **Flock** links **separate accounts**. Each person keeps their own account, but the costs go to one
  paying account.

## Members

Manage members under **Profile → Members**.

### Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and managing members. |
| **Billing** | Invoices, payment method and usage, plus everything a Member can do. |
| **Member** (default) | Everyday product use. No billing and no member management. |

Everyone on the account can see who belongs to it. Only owners can add, change or remove members.

### Add a member

<Steps>

1. Go to **Profile → Members**.
2. Type the person's **GitHub username** and choose a role.
3. Confirm. The person is added straight away. Zekt sends no invitation and no email, so let them
   know yourself.

</Steps>

Common messages when adding someone:

| Message | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| *GitHub has no user called …* | Check the spelling of the username. |
| *… is a GitHub organisation, not a person* | Members are people. Enter a personal GitHub username. |
| *We could not check that username with GitHub just now. Nothing has changed* | GitHub didn't answer. Try again shortly. |
| *already owns this account* / *already a … on this account* | The person is already on the account. Change their role instead. |

### Change or remove members

Use the filters **Active**, **Owners**, **Removed** and **All** to find people. Removed members stay
in the list as history.

To protect the account:

- The **last owner** can't be removed or demoted. Add another owner first.
- The **account owner**, the GitHub login the account belongs to, can't be removed or demoted on
  this screen.

Only owners can remove members. To leave an account, ask one of its owners to remove you.

### When someone leaves a GitHub organization

If someone is removed from a GitHub organization that has the Zekt GitHub App installed, Zekt removes
them from **every** Zekt account they're a member of. This applies however they were added, and to
all of their accounts, not only those connected to that organization. An owner can add them back.

There are two exceptions:

- An account's **only owner** stays, and the account gets a notice asking it to add another owner.
- The **account owner**, the GitHub login the account belongs to, is never removed this way.

Joining a GitHub organization never grants access to any Zekt account.

### Which account you act for

- If you own a Zekt account, you always act for that account.
- If you don't own an account but are a member of one, you act for that account.
- If you don't own an account and are an active member of **more than one**, Zekt can't tell which
  one you mean. You can sign in, but you aren't linked to any account and are told to contact
  [Zekt support](mailto:support@zekt.dev).

<Aside type="note" title="GitHub organization membership doesn't give access">
	Being a member of a GitHub organization gives you no access to any Zekt account, even if that
	account has repositories in the organization. An organization admin can't take over such an
	account.
</Aside>

## Flock: Shepherd and Disciple accounts

Use **Profile → Flock** when another person should keep their own Zekt account but have its costs
billed to yours. The paying account is the **Shepherd**. The linked account is a **Disciple**.

<Steps>

1. In the Shepherd account, go to **Profile → Flock** and create an invitation for the other
   person's **GitHub username**. You can restrict the link to a GitHub organization that you both
   belong to.
2. Send the link yourself. Zekt doesn't email it. It's valid for **7 days**.
3. When the person accepts:
	- their account becomes a Disciple account, and its costs are billed to the Shepherd;
	- they're also added as a member of the Shepherd's account.

</Steps>

A Shepherd can create and revoke [API tokens](/zekt-docs/operate/vscode-and-mcp/#api-tokens) for an
accepted Disciple.

A Shepherd can also limit what its Disciples connect to with [Doctrine](/zekt-docs/add-ons/shield/#doctrine-shepherd-accounts).

<Aside type="caution">
	Everything a Disciple account does is billed to the Shepherd. Only invite people you trust.
</Aside>
